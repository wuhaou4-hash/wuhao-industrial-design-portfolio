Project 01  /  Lighting

Garden

A collection of table lamps shaped like garden flowers, 3D printed in layered, translucent PLA. Light that grows out of care.

At the heart of the project is the image of a flower as the result of work, care and time.

灵感来自童年时家里的菜园与花园：每年由母亲和外婆照料，种植、除草、绑枝。这种不起眼却持续的劳动，随时间变成了美。灯具把这个过程变成隐喻，由照料而生的光，让空间充满温暖与舒适。

Collection
Narcissus, Poppy, Tulip
Making
FDM 3D printing, PLA, fuzzy skin
Light source
Smart E14 bulb
Shade
Three nested shades, up to Ø 215 mm

Concept

A flower is what time and care leave behind.

The collection looks back at the family dacha garden of my childhood, which my mother and grandmother tended every year: planting, weeding, tying up, looking after. That unnoticed but constant labour slowly turns into beauty.

The lamps carry this process as a metaphor. Light is born from care and fills the room with warmth and a feeling of comfort.

The forms are inspired by the narcissus, the poppy or peony, and the tulip. The key images come from the flowers most often found in a garden: tulips, poppies, peonies, dandelions, irises and narcissi.

Mana Fabric styleDevelop a collection of interior lamps in the Mana Fabric style.
Within reachMake them affordable, in both price and quality, for the Russian market.
Meaning in the objectCombine function, meaning and aesthetics in one design.

这个系列回望我童年时家里的田园花园：母亲和外婆每年都要照料，播种、除草、绑枝、悉心看护。这些不起眼却持续不断的劳动，慢慢变成了美。灯具把这一过程作为隐喻：光源于照料，让房间充满温暖与舒适。造型灵感来自水仙、罂粟（或牡丹）和郁金香。

Reference

Moodboard

The moodboard collects the photographs that set the look of the project: stylised flower illustrations, textured photographs of stems, and translucent materials that scatter light.

Form

Sketching

Sketching was a search for form and character. I settled on an elongated table lamp with a simplified flower shape.

草图是对形态与性格的探索，最终我选定了一款细长的台灯，配以简化的花形。

Test

Prototyping

Mock-ups were printed on a home 3D printer in several kinds of PLA. The lamp went through a number of iterations, and both the structure of the assembly and the overall form changed along the way.

模型用家用 3D 打印机、以多种 PLA 材料打印。灯具经历了多次迭代，装配结构和整体造型都在过程中发生了变化。

Result

Three flowers, three lamps

Each lamp has its own colour and its own flower. The shape stays simple: a slim stem, a ruffled bloom, a glowing centre.

每盏灯有自己的颜色和花。造型保持简洁：细长的花茎、褶皱的花朵、发光的花心。

Project 02  /  Terrace furniture  /  Annki studio

Mar

The Mar collection (“mar” is Portuguese for “sea”) is inspired by the atmosphere of the Portuguese and Greek coasts, the movement of waves and the feeling of living water.

The smooth curves of the metal frame recall the lines of a sea current, while soft volumes create a sense of calm and comfort. Tactile textures and a light structure form a place to rest, filled with light, air and a seaside mood.

Mar（葡萄牙语“海”）系列的灵感来自葡萄牙和希腊海岸的氛围、海浪的律动和流动之水的感觉。金属框架柔和的曲线呼应洋流的线条，柔软的体量营造出平静与舒适。触感的材质和轻盈的结构，构成一个充满光线、空气与海滨气息的休憩之所。

Collection
Lounge chair, footstool, screen, coffee table
Brief
Terrace furniture for Annki studio
Frame
Round metal tube, powder coated
Soft parts
Removable cushions in outdoor fabric

Brief

Furniture for resting outdoors

The task of this module was to design terrace furniture for Annki studio. I chose the lounge direction: furniture for relaxing in the open air.

The inspiration was the aesthetics and atmosphere of Portugal, long beaches, the sea breeze and giant waves. All of this led me to the form language of the collection.

The photographs show collages I assembled to find the visual language and to convey the mood of the project.

这个模块的任务是为 Annki 工作室设计露台家具，我选择了休闲（lounge）方向：适合在户外放松的家具。灵感来自葡萄牙的美学与氛围：绵长的海滩、海风和巨浪，由此引出了系列的造型语言。照片中的拼贴，是我为寻找视觉语言、传达项目氛围而制作的。

Research

What the market does

While researching terrace furniture from a range of Russian brands, I found recurring design patterns in the products on sale.

They are often made of metal with a powder-coated finish, built as an open frame, with removable parts in the form of cushions.

调研俄罗斯多个品牌的露台家具时，我发现在售产品中反复出现一些设计模式：多为粉末涂层金属，开放式框架，配有可拆卸的坐垫。

Form

Sketching

Pages of sketches exploring waves, whales, rolled cushions and flowing frames. Pink stars mark the favourites.

一页页草图探索了海浪、鲸鱼、卷起的坐垫和流动的框架，粉色星标是入选的方案。

早期概念效果图与最终方案的对比，之后是基于 3D 模型的神经网络草图渲染。

Neural-network sketch visualisations based on 3D models.

躺椅、脚凳、屏风和咖啡桌，采用圆管粉末涂层框架和软坐垫，这里展示了多种配色。

Project 03  /  Wearable IoT  /  Anosmia

Noseee

Noseee is a compact helper for smell-related situations at home. There is no universal, compact device on the market made specifically for everyday help for people with anosmia.

There is potential for a hybrid IoT device with several sensors and a mobile interface: a device that recognises unpleasant smells and warns the wearer, a small companion for daily routines.

Noseee 是一款在家庭场景中辅助嗅觉的小型助手。市场上缺少专门面向失嗅症人群日常辅助的通用小型设备，而带有多个传感器和移动端界面的混合式 IoT 设备有这方面的潜力：识别难闻气味并提醒佩戴者，成为日常生活中的小帮手。

Type
Wearable IoT device, worn as a glove
User
People diagnosed with anosmia
Sensing
Gas sensors at the fingertips
Feedback
Short vibration, statistics in a web interface

Context

What is anosmia?

Anosmia is the complete loss of the ability to perceive smells. It can be congenital or acquired, for example after injuries, viral illnesses (including after COVID-19) or neurological disorders.

The device is meant to signal substances that give off a bad smell or point to a hazardous environment.

失嗅症是完全丧失嗅觉的能力，可以是先天的，也可以是后天获得的，例如外伤、病毒感染（包括新冠之后）或神经系统疾病。设备用于提示会散发异味、或指示危险环境的物质。

Gases to detect: hydrogen sulphide (H₂S), ammonia (NH₃) and others. 硫化氢、氨气等。

System

Architecture

The vibration motor and wireless charging sit on the wrist. The gas analysers are at the fingers. The battery and controller are on the back of the hand.

振动马达和无线充电位于手腕处，气体分析仪在指尖，电池和控制器在手背。

Scenario

How it is used

The hand in the glove is brought up to an object. When an unpleasant smell appears, the device gives a short vibration as an indicator. Analysis statistics arrive in a web interface.

戴手套的手靠近物体。出现难闻气味时，设备会发出一次短振动作为提示，分析统计数据则发送到网页界面。

Prototype

Built and tested

The prototype uses all the components of the device except the gas analysers, which were replaced with MQ-2 sensors, and the wireless charger, replaced with a Type-C charging module.

原型使用了设备的全部组件，只是把气体分析仪换成了 MQ-2 传感器，无线充电器换成了 Type-C 充电模块。

Visualisation

The finished idea

Renders of the glove and its wrist module, and the companion app that reports what the sensors pick up.

手套和腕部模块的渲染图，以及显示传感器检测结果的配套 App。

Project 04  /  Uninterruptible power supply  /  DKC

DKC UPS

An uninterruptible power supply for home and office that combines equipment protection with workspace organisation.

A modular storage system adapts to the user’s needs and helps keep the desk in order. The aluminium body removes heat efficiently, and the compact size lets the device sit even on a small desk.

面向家庭和办公室的不间断电源（UPS），把设备保护和工作空间整理结合在一起。模块化收纳系统可根据用户需求调整，帮助保持桌面整洁；铝制机身高效散热，小巧的尺寸让它即使在有限的桌面空间也能摆放。

Product
UPS for home and office, for DKC
Size
300 × 220 × 100 mm
Body
Aluminium, satin finish
Buttons
Polycarbonate, RAL 9022, matte

Concept

Protection that tidies the desk

Most UPS units are hidden under the table. This one is designed to stay on it: a small aluminium box that protects the equipment and turns its side into a place for everyday things.

Two versions: brushed aluminium and black.

大多数 UPS 都藏在桌下，而这一款被设计成放在桌面上：一个小巧的铝制盒子，既保护设备，又把侧面变成放日常物品的地方。有铝色和黑色两种版本。

Form

Compact, and good at shedding heat

The body is aluminium, which carries heat away efficiently. Small dimensions let the unit fit even on a limited desk surface. A patterned vent runs along the side.

Overall size is 300 × 220 × 100 mm (length × height × depth).

铝制机身能高效导热，小巧的尺寸使它即使在有限的桌面上也能放得下，侧面有图案化的散热孔。整体尺寸为 300 × 220 × 100 mm（长 × 高 × 宽）。

Back panel: fan, two Schuko and two IEC outlets, mains inlet, USB and network ports, reset button.

Interface

Clear indication

A single status light on the top panel lets the user read the state of the device at a glance. Two USB-A and two USB-C ports, a power button and a mute button sit next to it.

顶部面板上的一盏状态灯，让用户一眼读懂设备状态；旁边是两个 USB-A、两个 USB-C 接口，以及电源键和静音键。

Status light

What the colours mean

Green, steady
Mains power; battery charging or already charged
Yellow, steady
Power has gone out, running on battery; audible alarm sounds
Yellow, flashing
Battery is low
Red, steady
Overload: too many devices connected

绿灯常亮：市电供电，电池充电中或已充满；黄灯常亮：断电，由电池供电，并有声音提示；黄灯闪烁：电池电量低；红灯常亮：过载，连接的设备过多。

Materials

Finish and details

Satin-finished aluminium body, matte polycarbonate buttons in RAL 9022, and a laser-engraved logo.

缎面处理的铝制机身，哑光聚碳酸酯按键（RAL 9022），激光雕刻的标志。

System

Modular storage

A perforated side panel is fixed to the body with pins, and hooks, a shelf and a pen cup clip into its slots. The user chooses what to keep within reach: headphones, cables, glasses, pens, notes.

带孔的侧板用销钉固定在机身上，挂钩、搁板和笔筒卡入槽中。用户可以自选要放在手边的东西：耳机、数据线、眼镜、笔、便签。
